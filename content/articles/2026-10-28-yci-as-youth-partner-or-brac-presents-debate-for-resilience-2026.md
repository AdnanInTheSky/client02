Youth Climate Insights (YCI) is proud to have served as a Youth Partner of BRAC Presents Debate for Resilience 2026, a national inter-university debate competition organised by the Debating Wing of the Institute of Disaster Management and Vulnerability Studies (IDMVS), University of Dhaka, in collaboration with BRAC’s Disaster Risk Management Programme.

Held on 12 October 2026 at the Muzaffar Ahmed Chowdhury Auditorium, University of Dhaka, the event marked the International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction under the theme “Safer Homes, Stronger Tomorrow.”

The competition brought together university debaters to critically explore the role of structural and institutional reforms versus individual awareness in reducing disaster risks. The Department of Development Studies, University of Dhaka, emerged as champion, while the Bangladesh University of Textiles Debating Club secured the runner-up position. Israt Jahan Ivee was recognised as Best Speaker of the Final, and Ahnaf Amir Aunabil received the Debater of the Tournament award.

The event also featured an insightful panel discussion titled “Transboundary Hazards, Localising Actions: Bridging the Gap in Preparedness and Response,” moderated by Professor Dr Mahbuba Nasreen.

The distinguished panellists included:

🔹 Kazi Amdadul Hoque — Senior Director of Strategic Planning and Head of Climate Action, Friendship

🔹 Hasin Jahan — Regional Director, WaterAid South Asia

🔹 ASM Marjan Nur — Climate and Environment Adviser, British High Commission

🔹 Md Ziaul Haque — Additional Director General, Department of Environment

The discussion highlighted the importance of disaster-resilient housing, inclusive preparedness, climate finance, technology, local leadership, and stronger institutional coordination, particularly for marginalised and hard-to-reach communities.

At YCI, we believe that empowering young people to engage in critical dialogue, exchange knowledge, and contribute to solutions is essential to building a safer, more inclusive, and climate-resilient Bangladesh.

As a Youth Partner, we are honoured to support a platform that connects youth voices with the broader conversations on disaster risk reduction and resilience. We have reached over 20 campuses and almost 200 participants registered.

We look forward to continuing our efforts to advance youth engagement, climate action, and community resilience.

Congratulations to all the participating teams, winners, organisers, panellists, and partners who contributed to making this initiative a success!
